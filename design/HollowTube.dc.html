Hollow out the tube

Solid bar weighs 227 pounds. Real tube is hollow.

1

Start a new sketch on the XY plane.

2

In its properties, set the attachment offset so the sketch sits just below the top face.

3

Draw two rectangles marking the inside of the tube walls.

4

Click Pocket and cut down, stopping just above the bottom face.

You should see

No change on screen. Check the weight instead — it drops from 227 pounds to 82.
