Where things are

Five parts of the window

Learn these five names and every instruction in this course tells you exactly where to look.

  • Menu bar. The last menu is named after whichever workbench you are in.
  • Toolbars. The same commands as pictures. Hover for the name.
  • Model tree, on the left. Everything in your file.
  • 3D view, in the middle. Your part.
  • Task panel. Appears in place of the tree when a command needs your input.
The FreeCAD Window02 / 08

The most important panel

The tree is your build history

FreeCAD does not save your part as a shape. It saves the recipe, and replays it whenever something changes.

  • Body is one solid lump of metal.
  • Origin holds the three starting planes. Select it and press Space to see them.
  • Sketches and features are listed in the order you made them.
  • A red mark on an icon means that step failed. Fix it before you carry on.

The task panel and the tree share the same space. The first time you open a Pad dialog the tree seems to vanish. It has not — click the Model tab at the bottom.

Two halves of one toolbar

Adding metal and removing metal

Every modelling command either adds material or takes it away. They work in pairs.

AddsRemovesMade from
PadPocketa sketch, pushed straight
RevolutionGroovea sketch, spun about an axis
Additive LoftSubtractive Lofttwo or more sketches
Additive PipeSubtractive Pipea sketch pushed along a path

Dress-up is different

  • Fillet, Chamfer and Draft do not use a sketch.
  • They work on the faces and edges of the solid you already have.
  • That makes them fragile. Add them last, and use as few as you can.

On the frame we ask for a fillet that is physically impossible, and FreeCAD refuses. Deck 5 walks through it.
