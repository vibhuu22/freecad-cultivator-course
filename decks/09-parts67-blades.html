Parts 6 and 7: The Blades01 / 07

The one real gap

SolidWorks bends them. FreeCAD cannot.

What the tutorial does

  • Builds the blade flat.
  • Runs Flex › Bending to warp it around a radius.
  • Blade 02 is the same thing bent on 16 inches instead of 10.

FreeCAD has no Flex. Not in Part Design, not in Part, not in any add-on. If you went looking for the button, it is not there.

What we do instead

  • Build the blade already curved.
  • Draw an arc of the radius we want, and sweep the blade's cross-section along it.
  • Then cut the plan shape out of the curved plate.

This is not a workaround. It is the better model. The bend radius becomes a number you can name and change, instead of an operation applied afterwards.

Parts 6 and 7: The Blades02 / 07

The spine

The arc the blade is bent on

A single arc on the YZ plane. It starts flat at the cutting edge and lifts towards the back.

  1. Draw an arc roughly where the blade goes
  2. Position its start point from the origin
  3. Dimension the radius from the spreadsheet10 in
  4. Force the arc's centre directly above its start, so the blade leaves the cutting edge flat
  5. Position the end point

We measure the blade's 4 inch length around the curve, not straight across. That is how much steel it takes, so that is the number to fix.

Parts 6 and 7: The Blades03 / 07

The sweep

Sweep the section along it

Same tool as the clamps: Additive Pipe.

  • The section is a plain rectangle, 13 inches by 0.2 inches.
  • It sits on the XZ plane, offset forward to the start of the arc.
  • Sweep it, and you have a curved plate.

Parts 6 and 7: The Blades04 / 07

The plan shape

Cutting the duckfoot shape

A sweep comes to a point in the middle and its edges rake back, so soil and trash slide off.

  • The cutting edges sweep back at 13 degrees.
  • Over a 6.5 inch half-width, 13 degrees lifts the outer corners 1.5 inches.
  • The tutorial separately quotes a 1.5 inch gap. Two numbers, same answer — so we have read it right.

To cut everything outside a shape, draw a big rectangle and the blade outline inside it, in one sketch. A pocket then removes the ring between them.

Parts 6 and 7: The Blades05 / 07

The payoff

Blade 02 is one number

Open the spreadsheet. Change the bend radius from 10 to 16. Recompute. That is the whole of Part 7.

  • The arc flattens, and the swept plate follows.
  • The plan-shape pocket re-cuts the same outline out of the new surface.
  • Same 13 inch span, same 4 inches of steel, same weight.

This is exactly the edit the SolidWorks tutorial makes by re-running Flex. Here it costs one cell — which is the whole argument for building it curved.

What we left out

Two things a real sweep has that this model does not.

  • Bolt holes. The tutorial never dimensions them, so we did not guess. That is exercise 4.
  • A ground edge. A real sweep is sharpened underneath along the leading edges.
